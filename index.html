Tor hides the content. People leave traces anyway.

Investigators have identified people using Tor without breaking its encryption. This site walks through how, one network layer at a time.

    Follow a request through guard, middle, and exit relays, then see what each one can and can’t see.

  2. 2. Case files

    Four real investigations as evidence boards. Decide for yourself whether Tor was actually broken.

  3. 3. Correlation lab

    Play the attacker. Match traffic going into Tor with traffic coming out, then add defenses.

  4. 4. Profile the suspect

    Work two fictional evidence lockers and find the layer that leaked.

Before you start: you should know the four TCP/IP layers, what an IP address is, and roughly how encryption works. Tor terms are explained the first time they appear, and every one is in the glossary.
